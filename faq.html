Frequently asked questions

Is JaMi a magic FPS booster?

No. JaMi is designed around game-specific evidence, hardware context and measurable changes.

Does JaMi disable Windows Defender?

The product direction is to keep Defender enabled, not to market disabling security as an optimization.

Is there a public installer?

Not yet. The website currently presents the Preview and research structure.
