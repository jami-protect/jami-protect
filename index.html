Why JaMi

Built around proof, safety and the actual game.

Instead of treating every PC and every game the same, JaMi Protect is designed around game context, current research and the hardware in front of it.

01

Game-specific knowledge

Research is organized by canonical game identity so recommendations stay attached to the correct title and platform.

02

Hardware-aware decisions

Recommendations can be adapted to CPU, GPU, VRAM, memory and other system constraints instead of relying on a universal preset.

03

Safe workflow first

Where changes are applied, JaMi is being designed around preview, backup, confirmation and restore paths rather than blind tweaking.
