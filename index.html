Why optimize?

Not everything running is bad. JaMi finds what actually matters.

Instead of treating Windows like “bloat” that must be stripped, JaMi is designed to identify real pressure around the active game, explain the tradeoff and only recommend changes that fit the current hardware and workload.

Core features

Short names. Clear outcomes.

Technical depth is available, but the first scan should tell a gamer exactly what JaMi is trying to improve.

01

Boost FPS Smarter

Use game-specific evidence and hardware context instead of a universal preset or fixed percentage claim.

02

Smooth Frame Times

Look beyond average FPS and focus on consistency, 1% lows and pacing where benchmark evidence exists.

03

Cut Background Pressure

Identify browsers, launchers, startup apps and other pressure without assuming every process must be killed.

04

Match Your Hardware

CPU, GPU, VRAM and memory context change which recommendation makes sense for this PC.

05

Protect Every Change

Preview risk, prepare backup/restore context and separate read-only guidance from real system changes.

06

Keep Research Fresh

Revisit game knowledge as patches, drivers and performance findings change instead of treating old advice as permanent truth.

JaMi focuses on

Things we can explain.

Game-specific settings and known performance behavior.
Hardware-aware recommendations and workload context.
FPS, frametimes and 1% lows where measurement is available.
Backup, restore and rollback requirements before risky changes.
JaMi avoids

Marketing shortcuts.

Easy-lobby or matchmaking manipulation tricks.
Anti-cheat bypasses or undocumented competitive-game hacks.
Mystery “AI routing” claims that do not explain what changed.
Universal “+45% / +50% FPS” guarantees across every PC.
