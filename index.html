Why JaMi?

Not everything running is bad. JaMi finds what actually matters.

Instead of treating Windows like “bloat” that must be stripped, JaMi is designed to identify real pressure around the active game, explain the tradeoff and recommend only changes that fit the current hardware and workload.

Core features

Short names. Clear outcomes.

01

Boost FPS Smarter

Use game-specific evidence and hardware context instead of a universal preset.

02

Smooth Frame Times

Look beyond average FPS and focus on 1% lows, pacing and consistency.

03

Cut Background Pressure

Identify browsers, launchers and startup apps without blindly killing processes.

04

Match Your Hardware

CPU, GPU, VRAM and memory context change which recommendation makes sense.

05

Protect Every Change

Preview risk and prepare backup / restore context before real system changes.

06

Keep Research Fresh

Revisit advice as games, drivers and Windows change.
