Anti-snake-oil positioning

Performance tools should earn your trust.

JaMi is deliberately positioned against unsafe “magic boost” utilities.

What JaMi aims to do

  • Research the exact game and separate sibling titles correctly.
  • Match recommendations to real hardware.
  • Measure FPS, frametimes and 1% lows where benchmarks are available.
  • Explain risk level before a system change.
  • Prepare backup and rollback paths where applicable.

What JaMi avoids

  • Universal “+45% FPS” guarantees.
  • Disabling Windows Defender for a performance claim.
  • Anti-cheat bypasses or undocumented competitive-game hacks.
  • Blind registry scripts with no explanation or restore path.
  • Calling generic YouTube advice game-specific evidence.
