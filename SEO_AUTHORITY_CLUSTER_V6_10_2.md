## Architecture

`Homepage → Guides hub → intent-specific guide → game profiles → related guide cluster`

Every authority route is available in EN, NL, DE, FR, ES, PT-BR, PL, JA and TR with reciprocal hreflang + x-default, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, WebPage/Breadcrumb/FAQ JSON-LD, and sitemap coverage.

## Content policy

- No universal FPS promises.
- No keyword stuffing.
- Crash/BSOD pages diagnose and explain; they do not claim to automatically repair every system failure.
- Game research remains evidence-ready until JaMi validation supports stronger recommendations.
- Search intent is separated across routes to reduce keyword cannibalization.
