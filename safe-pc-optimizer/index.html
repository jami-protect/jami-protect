Diagnose • Explain • Optimize • Recover

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does JaMi promise a specific FPS gain?

No. Performance depends on the game, patch, hardware, drivers, settings and workload. JaMi is designed to measure context and explain recommendations rather than promise a universal percentage.

Does JaMi disable Windows Security or anti-cheat?

The product direction is to preserve Windows security protections and avoid anti-cheat bypasses. High-risk or game-integrity changes belong behind explicit safety gates, not one-click optimization.

Why use game-specific pages as well?

Because engines, patches, renderers and settings behave differently. A game-specific profile gives the general Windows diagnosis the context it needs.
